Meet Detective Alex Morgan

Every mystery starts with a question. Alex stays until every clue has an answer.

The Detective

Alex Morgan is an independent investigator known for patience, careful observation, and an unusual ability to notice what others miss. Alex began keeping case files after solving a disappearance at the Bellweather Library.

Alex believes that every witness has a perspective, every clue has a context, and small details can change the whole story.

Investigation Skills

  • Observing details others overlook
  • Comparing timelines and witness statements
  • Connecting evidence to locations
  • Asking careful questions

Alex’s Rule

“Trust the evidence, but always check the details twice.”

Current Assignment

Alex is reviewing the open files in the archive, including The Missing Manuscript and The Forest Footprints.
